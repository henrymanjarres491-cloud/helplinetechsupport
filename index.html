What we will and won't do on a call

Scammers pose as tech support every day. Here's exactly where we draw the line, so you always know it's really us.

We verify identity first

Before touching a password or account, we confirm your name and phone number against what's on file. If it doesn't match, we stop and figure out why.

We never ask for payment mid-call

If you didn't set up billing with us ahead of time, we won't ask for a card number, gift cards, or a wire transfer during a support call. Anyone who does isn't us.

We won't push for remote access

Most fixes happen just by talking. If a problem genuinely needs someone to see your screen, we'll explain why, and it's always your call whether to allow it.

You can always hang up and call back

If anything about a call feels off, hang up and dial (702) 469-8064 yourself. A real support line will never mind you double-checking.

If someone calls claiming to be us and asks for money, a gift card, or remote access out of nowhere, it isn't us. Hang up and call the number on this page directly to confirm.

Helping a parent from a distance?

You're not always around when something breaks. Give them a number they can call themselves, one that's patient, honest, and never pressures them into anything.

Questions people ask

Yes. Describe what you're seeing or what stopped working. We'll ask a few questions and narrow it down from there.

Will you need to see my screen?

Rarely. Most calls get solved just by talking. If we ever think it's needed, we'll explain why and it's entirely up to you.

What if it's something you can't fix by phone?

We'll tell you plainly and point you toward the right next step instead of stringing the call along.

How do I know it's really you calling me back?

If you're ever unsure, hang up and dial (702) 469-8064 yourself. That number always reaches us directly.

Privacy & Terms

Privacy Policy

When you call, we collect your name and phone number so we can help you and follow up if needed. If your call involves a password or account, we also confirm your name and phone number against what's already on file before helping, as part of keeping your accounts safe.

We use this information only to help solve the problem you called about. We don't record calls, we don't sell or share your information with anyone else, and we don't access your accounts, install anything, or view your screen without your say-so on that specific call.

If you're calling on behalf of a parent or relative, we'll also note that relationship so we know who we're helping.

Questions about your information? Call (702) 469-8064 and ask.

Terms of Service

HelpLine Home Tech provides phone-based tech support. We don't make in-person visits, and we never take remote access to a device without asking first, every time.

The first call is free. Every call after that is a flat $20, regardless of how long it takes. Payment is arranged directly with us.

We do our best to walk you through a fix, but we can't guarantee every issue is solvable over the phone. Some problems may need an in-person repair service, and we'll tell you plainly if that's the case rather than stringing the call along.

We're not responsible for damage caused by mistakes made while following instructions, though we work carefully to make sure directions are clear before you act on them. We can decline or end a call for any reason, including abusive behavior toward our team.

These terms may be updated from time to time. Last updated September 2026.
