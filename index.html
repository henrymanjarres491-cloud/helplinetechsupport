Staying safe

What we will and won't do on a call

Scammers pose as tech support every day. Here's exactly where we draw the line, so you always know it's really us.

We verify identity first

Before touching a password or account, we confirm your name and phone number against what's on file. If it doesn't match, we stop and figure out why.

We never ask for payment mid-call

If you didn't set up billing with us ahead of time, we won't ask for a card number, gift cards, or a wire transfer during a support call. Anyone who does isn't us.

We won't push for remote access

Most fixes happen just by talking. If a problem genuinely needs someone to see your screen, we'll explain why, and it's always your call whether to allow it.

You can always hang up and call back

If anything about a call feels off, hang up and dial (702) 469-8064 yourself. A real support line will never mind you double-checking.

If someone calls claiming to be us and asks for money, a gift card, or remote access out of nowhere, it isn't us. Hang up and call the number on this page directly to confirm.
